# Born Saved Reimagined

I need you to rebuild the website for Born Saved (https://www.bornsaved.org/) using the Litera template kit as the design base:
Template link: https://templates.sparklethings.com/litera/template-kit/home/?storefront=envato-elements
Technical Requirements:

React + Vite
Clean, modular, reusable components with a sensible folder structure
Fully responsive (mobile / tablet / desktop)
Optimized for performance, accessibility, and SEO
Smooth animations (Framer Motion preferred)

Design Requirements:

Use the Litera template structure and modern layout as the foundation.
Color scheme:
Primary color: Dark Navy Blue (same as the header on the old site)
Accent color: Gold (exact gold from the logo)
Keep the overall look clean, elegant, professional, and faith-based.

Keep the logo from the old site (gold circular logo with cross).

Navigation (Very Important):
Keep the exact same menu items and names as the old website. Do not add or remove any tabs.
Menu must be:

Home
Shop
Blog
Further Reading
Why We Exist

Also include the Cart icon in the header.
Content Requirements:
Use the complete content and images from the old website (https://www.bornsaved.org/). Do not invent new content.
1. Home Page

Hero section with the main message:
“One Gospel. One Savior. One finished work.”
Supporting text about salvation beginning with what God has done in Christ.
CTA button: “Explore The Gospel”

Section explaining the purpose of Born Saved.org
Collection Highlights / Featured Books section showing the books

2. Shop Page
Show all products from the old site:

Born Saved: Rediscovering God's Accomplished Work In Christ
The Day of Judgement: Two Goats and the Final Removal of Sin
When The Center Shifted: How Christianity Kept The Words But Changed The Story
The Seal of God or the Mark: The Choice That Will Divide The World
Born Saved Witnessing Pamphlets

Use the original book covers and descriptions.
3. Blog Page
Keep the same introduction text:
“Open the Bible. Ask the hard questions. Look again at Christ...”
4. Further Reading Page
Transfer the complete list of recommended resources and authors (Jack Sequeira, Anders Nygren, John Stott, Harry Johnson, Oscar Cullmann, Hans K. LaRondelle, Edward Fudge, Ranko Stefanovic, etc.) with their descriptions.
5. Why We Exist Page
Use the full mission content including the Bible references (Matthew 24:14, Revelation 14:6-7, Daniel 8) and the explanation of why the ministry exists.
Additional Instructions:

Maintain a clean, readable, and trustworthy design suitable for a Gospel/teaching ministry.
Keep the tone respectful, serious, and Christ-centered.
Do not add extra pages or menu items that are not on the original site.
Prioritize clarity and good typography.

Please build the complete website following the above requirements as closely as possible.
